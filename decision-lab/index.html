Project Purpose

Marketing Decision Lab · Lucy Nowacki

A practical demonstration of how commercial data becomes a decision that a team can question, test and review.

The business questionWhere should a cab business investigate growth, and what evidence is needed before changing its marketing budget?

What the project brings together

Python analysis of 359,392 historical cab trips; customer and city comparisons; forecasting checked against a simple baseline; and a separate simulated marketing-budget experiment. A Jira-style board connects the findings to owners, acceptance checks and human review.

A suggested route through the project

  1. 01OverviewFilter company, city and year. Compare revenue with recorded gross contribution.
  2. 02Customer segmentsExplore repeat-trip behaviour and frame a retention experiment.
  3. 03Model validationCompare forecasts on unseen months. See why the simpler baseline wins here.
  4. 04Media labAdjust a simulated budget and channel shares to explore diminishing returns.
  5. 05Decision boardOpen a task, inspect its evidence and complete the review checks before marking it Done.
  6. 06Evidence & methodsCheck sources, assumptions and limitations. Use Export brief for a stakeholder summary.
How to read the results

CAB is a historical educational dataset in USD, not current client data. The media experiment is entirely simulated in GBP. Neither demonstrates real campaign lift. Review decisions are stored in this browser.

Where are the differences?

Known billed-minus-expected amounts, including completed inspections. These are not recovered savings.

Inspection workload

Latest saved decision per flagged invoice line. Completed means confirmed error or valid charge.

Visit outcomes

Unique service records, not invoice lines. A duplicated charge does not add a visit.

Reasons for investigation

Detected issue counts. One line can have several issues, so counts can exceed flagged lines.

Review decisions

Share of flagged lines by latest saved decision.

Visits over time

Daily recorded visits and completed visits by service date. Missing dates within the recorded range count as zero recorded visits.
